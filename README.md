# DSA210 Term Project
## Identifying High-Potential but Under-Engaged Customers Through Education, Income, Campaign Behavior, and Macroeconomic Context

---

## Research Question
Among highly educated customers (PhD, Master's), spending is highest yet campaign responsiveness is lowest. This project investigates whether education level, relative income, and the macroeconomic environment at enrollment (CPI) can explain this paradox and help identify high-potential but under-engaged customer segments.

## Hypothesis Tests

| # | Test | Method |
|---|------|--------|
| H1 | Education level × campaign engagement | Chi-square |
| H2 | PhD vs. non-PhD total spend | Mann-Whitney U |
| H3 | PhD engaged vs. non-engaged spend | Mann-Whitney U |
| H4 | PhD engaged vs. non-engaged relative income | Mann-Whitney U |
| H5 | CPI at enrollment × campaign engagement | Mann-Whitney U |
| H6 | Campaign score across all education groups | Kruskal-Wallis + post-hoc |
